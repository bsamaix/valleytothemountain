The Car

The Huracán

A 2017 Lamborghini Huracán — and the most unlikely mentorship tool you'll ever meet.

Add your hero photo → assets/huracan-hero.jpg

Why a supercar?

The door-opener

Let's be honest — a Lamborghini gets attention. And for a young man who has tuned out every adult who ever tried to “mentor” him, that attention is the invitation. The Huracán is the reason he says yes to the ride.

Once the doors scissor up and the V10 lights, the car fades into the background and the conversation takes over. No classroom. No audience. Just two people, an open road, and the freedom to be honest. The car gets him in the seat; God does the rest.

  • It opens doors a sit-down “talk” never could
  • It levels the field — wonder and excitement, not judgment
  • It creates a moment a young man remembers for life
  • It funds the mission — every mile points back to the cause

Every mile has a mission

This isn't a trophy in a garage. The Huracán works for a living — and the math is simple: the attention it earns becomes views, the views become support, and the support becomes rides for young men and seed money for a trade-school non-profit.

The odometer keeps climbing because the mission keeps moving. Every one of those 34,000+ miles is pointed uphill — from the valley toward the mountain.
