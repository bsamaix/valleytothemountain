The Show

“Valley to the Mountain”

A YouTube show where the conversations matter more than the car — even when the car is a Lamborghini.

Each episode, host Bobby Samai picks up a young man from the Willamette Valley and goes for a 1–2 hour drive. No script. No agenda except honesty. We talk about life, problems, family, plans, faith, and the road ahead — and we let the conversation go wherever it needs to go.

A day on set

What a ride looks like

1

The pickup

We meet at an agreed spot with the guardian present. Introductions, ground rules, and yes — a good look at the Huracán.

2

The drive

One to two hours through the valley. The conversation opens up naturally as the miles roll by.

3

The real talk

Family, struggles, faith, fears, and dreams. Anything truly private stays off-camera, between us.

4

The episode

Carefully edited with privacy protections, the encouraging parts become a video that helps other kids.

The conversation

What we actually talk about

There’s no curriculum. But over a couple of hours on the road, the real stuff always surfaces:

  • Family — the good, the broken, and everything in between
  • The pressures of being a young man today
  • Identity, purpose, and what to do with your life
  • Hard choices, mistakes, and second chances
  • Faith — who Jesus is, and why He changed everything for me
  • Practical plans: school, trade, work, and the next right step

Non-negotiable

Safety, privacy & trust come first

This show only works if parents trust it completely. Here is what that means in practice.

✓

Guardian consent

Every participant under 18 requires a parent or guardian to complete and sign the application, consent, and media-release forms before any filming.

🔒

Privacy protection

Identities and sensitive details are protected. Guardians review and approve how their young man is represented before anything is published.

🎬

Careful editing

Footage is edited responsibly. Anything personal, identifying, or sensitive that shouldn’t be public simply doesn’t make the cut.

🤝

Off-camera trust

The deepest conversations can happen off the record. A young man should feel safe being honest without a camera in his face.

🛡️

Accountable host

Bobby is committed to background checks and transparent practices, and welcomes guardian questions at any time.

↩️

Consent can be withdrawn

A guardian may decline to have an episode published. The young man’s wellbeing always outranks the content.

A note to parents: these are commitments and intentions for how the show operates. Please review every form carefully, ask any question you have, and consult your own advisors before signing. Bobby: have an attorney review all forms & minor-safety policies before launch.

Questions

Frequently asked

Who is the show for?

Young men ages 9–17 in Oregon's Willamette Valley — along the I-5 corridor from Eugene to Salem, and roughly 25 miles east and west of it — who could use a trustworthy mentor in their corner.

Does it cost anything to be on the show?

No. Being a guest is completely free. The show is funded by viewers and supporters, not by the families who participate.

Does a parent have to be involved?

Yes, always. A parent or guardian must apply, sign consent and media-release forms, and is welcome to ask questions throughout the process.

What if we don't want the episode published?

That's completely okay. Consent can be withheld or withdrawn, and a young man's wellbeing always comes before any video.

Is this a religious show?

Faith is a real and honest part of who Bobby is and what comes up on the drive, but no young man is pressured. The goal is genuine conversation, not a sales pitch.

How do we apply?

Head to the Apply page. A parent or guardian fills out the form, and Bobby will follow up personally.
