# Images for Valley to the Mountain

Drop your real photos in this folder using the **exact filenames** below and they
will appear automatically across the site (the site shows a tasteful gradient
placeholder until each file exists).

| Filename | Where it shows | Suggested shot |
|---|---|---|
| `huracan-hero.jpg` | Home hero background | The Huracán, wide, dramatic — leave room on the left for text. ~2000px wide. |
| `huracan-feature.jpg` | Home "A door-opener" section | Clean side/front shot of the car. |
| `valley-road.jpg` | Home "Safety & trust" section | An Oregon valley road / scenery. |
| `huracan-story.jpg` | Story page | You with the car, or a detail shot. |
| `show-interior.jpg` | Show page | Interior / in-car perspective (no minors' faces unless released). |

**Tips**
- Use `.jpg` for photos (smaller files). Aim for under ~400 KB each; compress at
  [squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app) if needed.
- Landscape orientation works best for these slots.
- Keep `logo.svg` as-is (it's the site logo & favicon), or replace it with your own.
- **Privacy:** never publish a recognizable photo of a minor without a signed media release from their guardian.
