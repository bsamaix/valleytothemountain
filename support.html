Support the mission

Fuel for the journey

A 12-month commitment with a vision that outlasts it — turning conversations into careers.

For the first 12 months, Bobby is committing fully to Valley to the Mountain. Every dollar the show earns is put to work in two stages: first keeping the Huracán on the road, then building something far bigger than a YouTube channel.

🚗

Stage one — keep it rolling

Show revenue first covers the Huracán’s payment and the real costs of production — fuel, insurance, editing, and the gear that makes each episode possible. No rides, no reach.

🎓

Stage two — build the non-profit

Beyond the car, funds go toward launching a non-profit that helps pay for trade-school programs for young men who choose that path after high school. A conversation today; a career tomorrow.
